# Formloom — Studio Strategy

1. **Who is this for?** Design-conscious homeowners and interior designers furnishing calm, lasting rooms — not impulse decor shoppers.
2. **What makes it unique?** A quiet architectural showroom: room chapters (Living / Sleep / Light), material truth (linen, walnut), and film-led storytelling instead of a generic product grid hero.
3. **First 5 seconds?** Warm showroom film, Fraunces typography, and a vertical chapter scroll — “I want this room,” not “another furniture site.”
4. **Signature interaction?** **Room Stage** — orbit the sofa in WebGL, swap upholstery swatches, toggle room mood (Living / Sleep / Light) with daylight vs evening light.
5. **Primary journey?** Hero film → room chapters / planner → PDP (dims + materials) → white-glove cart → coupon checkout → success.
6. **Layout system?** Split hero (sticky copy + snap-scroll chapters), forest/clay/limestone palette, DM Sans body via Sora, sharp borders — no pill clusters.
7. **3D role?** Purposeful sofa stage for material decisions; static room still when `prefers-reduced-motion`.
8. **What we avoid?** Beige AI templates, identical card grids, purple gradients, bolt-on 3D widget on a thin catalog.
